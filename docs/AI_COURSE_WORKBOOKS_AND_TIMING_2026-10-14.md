# Course workbooks, video resources and duration correction — 14 October 2026

All 53 existing introductory course packs now have a lesson-1 study guide and two optional YouTube channel references with topic suggestions. Lesson 6 contains six reusable documents: project brief and acceptance criteria, work/time plan, source and AI-assistance log, test/correction record, draft rubric, reflection/handover. The existing course-specific project brief is embedded in each workbook.

The catalogue previously included planned durations of up to 8,640 minutes despite the current six-lesson packs. Current estimates are 60–480 active minutes depending on the actual introductory task and prerequisites. Reading, exercises, one project and self-checking are included. Optional videos, setup, prerequisite catch-up and waiting for feedback are excluded and stated separately. Estimates are editorial planning budgets, not measured completion statistics; learner timing is requested in the workbook.

The separate 52-unit foundation is now self-paced, with 45–180 minutes per unit and 49 total estimated active hours instead of 208. One unit per week is optional. Both the in-app reader and static handbook use the updated estimates.

The reader renders standalone HTTPS resource links without executing HTML. The study pack download contains only the selected course's learner-visible lessons, workbook and resources. It does not export private teacher keys, submit work, mark completion or award certificates.
