What MELA is preparing

Children's safety

MELA requires age-appropriate access. A code or student-entered guardian contact does not prove guardian authority. An authorized reviewer must verify the relationship before protected child data is shared. Private school-stage work messaging remains restricted.

Personal data of minors must not be used for marketing or profiling under the applicable Ethiopian rules. A child may request a trusted adult's help with account rights.

About personal data

Depending on the service used, MELA may handle account and role details, education records, learning progress, verified professional evidence, guardian relationships, safety reports, and payment references. Final category-specific retention periods, local hosting obligations and international transfer arrangements require approved documentation.

Signed-in users can open My account or Safety & Privacy to submit private data-access, export, correction, erasure, objection or restriction requests. Requests require review and do not automatically erase data.

Payments and refunds

Pro and Premium plan descriptions, referral rewards, proof-of-payment review, subscription activation and refund handling must be checked against the live merchant and provider process before payments are enabled or advertised as available. A displayed tier does not prove that a payment was received or that an entitlement was activated.

Remaining release checks

Before unrestricted use, MELA must resolve the Ethiopia-local data hosting position and legal registration requirements, publish independently reviewed policies and accurate business contacts, approve minor safeguarding procedures, verify any paid-service handling, and pass user testing for all critical consent and deletion paths.
