Study without a connection

  1. Choose one handbook unit and read its explanation.
  2. Close the explanation and describe the idea in your own words.
  3. Try its practical task on paper using fictional information.
  4. Compare your work with the example and write down what to ask your teacher.
What still needs internet?

Sign-in, profile changes, AI Coach, live Arena matches, question-bank retrieval, applications, messaging, payments and teacher approvals need internet. This library does not queue submissions. Keep work in your own notebook and submit it after reconnecting.
